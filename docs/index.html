Nuclearer

Nuclear projects die on fatal siting constraints discovered late, not on land. Nuclearer surfaces them in week one: ranked, scored, cited.

An interactive 3D globe plus a screen-level site-screening engine for SMR, coal-to-nuclear repower, and large new-build pathways.

The problem

Land is not the bottleneck. The site readiness bundle is.

Nuclear projects are delayed or killed less by the availability of land and more by the availability of a site readiness bundle. Teams waste months pursuing sites that fail on one or two fatal constraints discovered late.

Grid interconnection realityqueue position, upgrade capex, timeline — lines nearby ≠ capacity
Cooling & water constraintsrights, discharge limits, drought and heat — water access isn't permission
Zoning & entitlementsrezoning likelihood, long and uncertain approval cycles
Environmental permitting triggersEIA/ESIA thresholds create schedule and litigation risk
Geohazardsseismic, flood, liquefaction, multi-hazard coupling — can be fatal late
Emergency planning practicalitypopulation, access, evacuation feasibility
Social & political acceptanceopposition and litigation risk can stall projects for years

SMR

Smaller footprints, modular logistics, flexible cooling — still subject to licensing, grid, and security realities.

Coal-to-nuclear repower

Reuses switchyard, water intakes, and industrial zoning, but carries legacy contamination, permits, and community politics.

Large new build

Larger footprints, stronger cooling needs, highest regulatory scrutiny — coastal/river sites and energy corridors.

Operational guardrail: Nuclearer never claims "permit-approved" or "licensable" results. Every output uses screen-level language, labels its assumptions, and attaches confidence and data provenance.

The product

Spin the globe. Pick a reactor. Screen the sites.

1

Interactive 3D globe

Click a region and the camera flies there. Selected regions highlight; a region roster gives one-click navigation.

2

Region context, cited

A floating glassmorphic dashboard shows expandable fact panels — land, legal, hazards — each linked to its source with a confidence level. Statutory bans raise an explicit alert.

3

Reactor catalog

12 reactor models across 7 families, each with footprint, cooling, and logistics specs, with citations.

4

Find Sites

A ranked shortlist per reactor+region combo: friction bars across grid, cooling, permits, community, and logistics; a per-constraint matrix with reasons and citations; a confidence level and a next-studies list.

5

Fatal-flaw detection

Regions with a statutory ban return no viable sites and say why, with the statute cited. A different reactor produces a different shortlist.

How it was built

LLM reasoning behind a curated cache, citations as a first-class type

React 18 TypeScript Vite globe.gl three.js Express OpenAI SDK react-markdown Vitest Playwright

Frontend

  • Globe — globe.gl + three.js, region selection, fly-to camera
  • Dashboard — region roster, fact panels, reactor picker, site finder, results modal
  • Design system — dark glassmorphism, pass/caution/fail verdict language, animated friction bars

Backend

  • Express API — site-finder orchestration
  • LLM reasoning path — OpenAI for uncached region+reactor combos
  • Curated-answer cache — vetted analyses served deterministically; the LLM is the fallback, so demos never depend on a live model call and vetted answers never drift

Citation backbone

  • Citations everywhere — every fact panel, matrix row, and reactor spec resolves through a citation layer
  • loadCorpus() seam — isolates where region legal text enters the system, built as the attachment point for a legal-RAG pipeline over real statute
  • Confidence levels — high / medium / low on every conclusion, plus a next-studies list
